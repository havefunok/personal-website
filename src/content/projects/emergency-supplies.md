## The purpose of the project

For my Eagle Scout service project, I worked on restoring emergency supplies at Mission San Jose Elementary School. The project involved rebuilding shelves, replacing expired supplies, and improving labeling and inventory. These tasks were connected by the need to make the supplies more useful to the people who would rely on them.

## Considering how the supplies would be used

Although replacing expired supplies addressed their immediate condition, their usefulness also depended on how they were stored and organized. Rebuilding the shelves addressed the physical storage, while labeling and inventory made it easier to understand what was available and where it belonged. Each part of the work therefore addressed a different part of how the same resource would be used.

This relationship was important because the supplies would continue to require attention after the project was finished. Clear organization could help the next person understand what needed to be checked, replaced, or returned to its proper place. Considering that later use was part of understanding what the project needed to accomplish.

## What I took from the experience

The project made me think more carefully about how the value of a piece of work extends into the way other people use it afterward. This is also relevant to how I approach engineering, because a system needs to be understandable to the people who will operate and maintain it. The work involved in making that possible is part of the design itself.
